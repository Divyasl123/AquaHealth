🧪 Common Minerals and Dissolved Ions

Calcium (Ca²⁺)Common mineral found in water; contributes to hardness.Individual value: Not measured
Magnesium (Mg²⁺)Common dissolved mineral; contributes to hardness.Individual value: Not measured
Sodium (Na⁺)Dissolved ion that may be present in natural or treated water.Individual value: Not measured
Potassium (K⁺)Another ion that can contribute to dissolved-solids content.Individual value: Not measured
Chloride (Cl⁻)Common dissolved ion, often present with sodium.Individual value: Not measured
Bicarbonate (HCO₃⁻)Common ion in groundwater and mineral water.Individual value: Not measured

Important: Your TDS sensor measures an overall estimated TDS value. It cannot tell how many ppm of calcium, magnesium, sodium or other individual minerals are present. Individual mineral concentrations require separate laboratory or ion-specific testing.
